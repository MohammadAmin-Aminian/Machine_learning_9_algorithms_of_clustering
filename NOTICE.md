# Provenance and modifications

This repository is a fork of material originally copyrighted by
**Mohamadhasan Sarvandani (2023)** under the MIT License. The original license
notice is preserved in `LICENCE.txt`.

Subsequent maintenance and educational additions by **Mohammad Amin Aminian**
include, among other changes:

- reproducible preprocessing and shared model interfaces;
- corrected categorical encoding and missing-value handling;
- deterministic regression tests and continuous integration;
- explicit treatment of clustering noise and undefined silhouette scores;
- an original synthetic geophysical feature-clustering lab;
- Adjusted Rand Index evaluation against known synthetic labels;
- teaching notes, package metadata and a command-line interface.

These additions do not change the provenance of the original forked material.
The repository should be cited and described as an educational adaptation and
extension, not as a claim of sole authorship of the original project.
